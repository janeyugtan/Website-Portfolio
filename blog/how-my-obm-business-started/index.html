How my OBM business started

By Jane YugtanNovember 19, 20263 min read

It all started with my portfolio.

One application, one portfolio

In October 2025, I applied to work with someone in an e-commerce co-founder’s network, who saw my work and knew right away he needed what I do. By February 2026, I was rebuilding his company’s Notion workspace into one system the whole team could actually use.

Four clients, one offer taking shape

Next came a video production company, where I stepped in part-time as their project manager and built out their project and deliverables tracking.

Then came a one-month retainer with a creative copywriting studio that wasn’t the right fit, and it taught me as much as the wins did.

Then a referral from that production company led me to the founder of a design consultancy, and that’s where I finally formalized my OBM services into an ongoing partnership, the offer I’m now building my 2027 investment guide around.

At a glance

  1. E-commerce brandRebuilt the company’s Notion workspace into one system the whole team uses.
  2. Video production companyPart-time project manager; built project and deliverables tracking.
  3. Creative copywriting studioA one-month retainer that wasn’t the right fit, and a lesson in what is.
  4. Design consultancyA referral, and where my OBM services became an ongoing partnership.

Not a juggle. Integration.

This work helps put food on our table alongside my main role as a client success manager, without sacrificing my time or the life I have right now.

It’s not a juggle anymore. It’s integration.

I’m an OBM, and I walk the talk: I help founders focus on their highest priorities, and I’m doing the same in my own life.
